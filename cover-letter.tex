Although the scale of the business was small, I utilised that information to forecast future orders and ensured that we maintained adequate materials for these predictions, which frequently proved accurate. Additionally, I recognised the importance of precise bookkeeping through identifying discrepancies in accounts and conducting thorough due diligence.

Since then, I have developed a career centred on applying quantitative and analytical rigor to real-world data. As a PhD candidate at Aalto University and a data analytics consultant, I have designed data pipelines, automated reporting workflows, and worked with extensive, detail-sensitive datasets where accuracy and consistency are paramount—skills that are directly applicable to finance operations involving vendors, customers, master data, and journal entries. I am adept at quickly learning new systems and take pride in being detail-oriented and reliable, qualities that I understand are essential for success within GFS's team environment.

I am fluent in English, have an intermediate proficiency in Finnish, and am a motivated, collaborative team player who thrives on learning the practical, large-scale finance processes that a global company like Neste relies upon. I am available to commence work on 1 September 2026 and would be keen to continue in the role through August 2027.

Thank you for considering my application. I would be pleased to discuss how my background in finance, data, and detail-oriented analytical work could contribute to your team.
