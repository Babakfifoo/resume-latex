Dear hiring manager at Norrin,

I am writing to apply for the data scientist role. As a former consultant at WSP Finland Oy and a PhD candidate at Aalto University, I bring 7+ years of experience across data engineering, econometrics, and applied statistics. I have a strong statistical and data science foundation and a track record of quantitative research and working on customer projects.  I’m finishing my degree, and therefore, I’m looking for my next step in my career.

My doctoral research applies econometrics to the housing market, urban planning and land development. The research is addressing the topic from a quantitative perspective in an area that has been mainly researched qualitatively.  I’ve extracted data from various sources of open data, PDF and HTML documents using local LLM frameworks such as Ollama, Hugging Face Transformers and LangChain. Furthermore, I designed and applied methods such as hedonic regression, survival analysis, and time series analysis for my research. I’m familiar with version controlling and replication using tools like Git and Docker. In addition to my research work, I’ve been the data owner for the quantitative real estate research data in my unit. In practice, I’ve modelled the data acquisition and processing, ensuring compliance with GDPR and Aalto policies, and provided support for other researchers in the use of the data. Besides my PhD work, I’ve done freelancing work as a data analytics engineer.

I thrive when I can solve complex problems, work with other people and continuously learn and develop my skills. In WSP, colleagues frequently sought my assistance with complex GIS data enquiries. Although my direct experience with mathematical optimisation frameworks is limited, my background in statistical modelling, econometrics, and problem-solving equips me to quickly master linear, nonlinear, and stochastic optimisation methods.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my analytical skills and engineering experience could contribute to your team.

Sincerely,
